# Plan C: "EMPIRE" - 24 Month Global Domination Strategy

## Overview
The Empire plan is an ambitious 24-month strategy designed to build a $10B+ revenue company that defines the future of work automation through global expansion, ecosystem development, and AI-powered innovation.

## Strategic Objectives

### Primary Goal
**$10B+ revenue company** defining the future of work automation

### Secondary Objectives
- **$1B ARR** by end of month 24
- **100,000 customers** globally
- **1,000 enterprise clients** with $100K+ ARR
- **40% international revenue** from global markets
- **60% ecosystem revenue** from platform and services
