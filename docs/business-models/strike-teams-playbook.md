# Strike Teams Playbook: Elite Enterprise Deployment Units

## Overview

Torqvio Strike Teams are elite, high-speed units that enter organizations, deploy automation, create immediate impact, and establish dependency. They replace traditional consulting with rapid, outcome-driven execution.

## Strike Team Philosophy

> **"We don't consult. We install leverage."**

Strike Teams operate on three principles:
1. **Speed Over Analysis** - Deploy in weeks, not months
2. **Impact Over Reports** - Measurable outcomes, not presentations  
3. **Dependency Over Satisfaction** - Critical integration, not happy clients
