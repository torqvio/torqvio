# Subscription-Based SaaS Business Model

## Overview
Torqvio's primary business model is a tiered subscription SaaS platform with usage-based components, designed to scale with customer success while providing predictable revenue.

## Pricing Tiers

### 1. Free Tier ($0/month)
**Target**: Entry-level users and developers getting started
**Strategy**: "Hook Them Forever" - eliminate friction to drive adoption

#### Features:
- Limited projects (1-3)
- Limited workflows (5-10)
- Basic execution limits (100/month)
- Community support
- 30-day log retention
- Basic retry policies

#### Business Logic:
- Lead generation for paid tiers
- Product familiarity and dependency building
- Network effects through user growth

### 2. Starter Tier ($25/month)
**Target**: Indie developers and small projects
**Strategy**: Impulse purchase price point with clear value

#### Features:
- Expanded projects (10)
- Extended workflows (50)
- Higher execution limits (1,000/month)
- Email support
- 90-day log retention
- Standard retry policies
- Webhook reliability

#### Business Logic:
- First revenue generation
- Habit formation around the platform
- Upgrade path preparation

### 3. Pro Tier ($99/month)
**Strategy**: "Real Businesses" - primary revenue driver
**Target**: Established businesses and production workloads

#### Features:
- Unlimited projects
- Advanced workflows (200)
- High execution limits (10,000/month)
- Priority email support
- 6-month log retention
- Advanced retry policies
- Priority queue execution
- Basic monitoring

#### Business Logic:
- Core revenue generation
- Customer retention through value
- Enterprise pipeline development

### 4. Business Tier ($399/month)
**Strategy**: "Critical Infrastructure" - enterprise-lite offering
**Target**: Growing companies with advanced needs

#### Features:
- Unlimited everything
- Unlimited executions (within fair use)
- Priority support with SLA
- 1-year log retention
- Advanced monitoring
- Team collaboration features
- Custom integrations

#### Business Logic:
- High-margin revenue
- Enterprise relationship building
- Platform lock-in through dependencies

### 5. Enterprise Tier (Custom Pricing)
**Strategy**: "We Run Your Company" - premium consulting
**Target**: Large enterprises with complex requirements

#### Features:
- Everything in Business tier
- Dedicated infrastructure
- Custom SLAs
- On-premise deployment options
- Professional services
- Training and onboarding
- Custom development

#### Business Logic:
- Highest margin revenue
- Strategic partnerships
- Market validation

## Usage-Based Overage Model

### Execution Pricing
- **Free/Starter**: $0.001 per execution (encourages upgrade)
- **Pro**: $0.0008 per execution (rewards commitment)
- **Business**: $0.0006 per execution (volume discount)
- **Enterprise**: $0.0004 per execution + step-run billing

### Business Logic
- Aligns revenue with customer success
- Provides scaling path for growing customers
- Creates predictable revenue patterns
- Reduces customer friction around limits

## Revenue Metrics & KPIs

### Primary Metrics
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Core revenue health
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**: Long-term revenue stability
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Customer profitability
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Marketing efficiency
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: Unit economics health (target > 3:1)

### Secondary Metrics
- **Monthly Churn Rate**: Customer retention (target < 5%)
- **Expansion Revenue**: Growth from existing customers
- **Net Revenue Retention**: Including upgrades and downgrades
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**: Revenue efficiency
- **Customer Satisfaction (NPS)**: Product-market fit

### Leading Indicators
- **Free Tier Activation**: User engagement quality
- **Time to First Value**: Product effectiveness
- **Feature Adoption Depth**: Platform dependency
- **Support Ticket Volume**: Product issues and opportunities

## Financial Projections

### Revenue Assumptions
- **Free to Paid Conversion**: 5-8% (industry average)
- **Starter to Pro Upgrade**: 20-30% annually
- **Pro to Business Upgrade**: 10-15% annually
- **Add-on Adoption**: 15-25% of paid customers
- **Annual Revenue Growth**: 100-200% (early stage)

### Cost Structure
- **Infrastructure Costs**: 20-30% of revenue
- **Customer Support**: 10-15% of revenue
- **Sales & Marketing**: 30-40% of revenue
- **R&D**: 25-35% of revenue
- **G&A**: 10-15% of revenue

### Profitability Timeline
- **Year 1**: Negative (investment phase)
- **Year 2**: Break-even (product-market fit)
- **Year 3**: Positive cash flow (scale phase)
- **Year 4+**: 30-40% margins (mature business)

## Risk Mitigation

### Market Risks
- **Competition**: Continuous innovation and ecosystem development
- **Market Adoption**: Free tier and developer-first approach
- **Pricing Pressure**: Value-based pricing and outcome demonstration

### Operational Risks
- **Infrastructure Scalability**: Cloud-native architecture and auto-scaling
- **Customer Support**: Tiered support model and self-service resources
- **Quality Assurance**: Automated testing and monitoring

### Financial Risks
- **Revenue Concentration**: Diversified customer base and usage-based pricing
- **Cash Flow**: Monthly billing and predictable revenue streams
- **Unit Economics**: Continuous LTV:CAC optimization

---

This subscription-based SaaS model provides a solid foundation for sustainable growth while maintaining flexibility for adaptive pricing and value-based monetization strategies.
